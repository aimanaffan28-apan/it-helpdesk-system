MPU 22153 • PROCESSES, PROCEDURES AND INSTRUCTIONS

Student Registration System

A workplace process improvement study that redesigns student registration from a paper-based, manually verified process into an integrated digital workflow.

Explore the Process ↓
A • SITUATION OVERVIEW

Why does registration need improvement?

🏫

Institution

An educational institution that manages student course registration through an academic department, advisers and an institutional database.

⚠️

Main Problem

The process depends on printing, manual verification, physical approval and repeated data entry. Peak periods can make the portal slow and create delays.

🎯

Expected Result

Students should complete registration accurately, without printed forms, with digital approval and immediate confirmation within 15 minutes.

C • STUDENT B

Possible Problems & Affected Parties

🐌

1. Portal delays

Consequence: Students may spend longer trying to register, especially during peak periods.

Affected: Students, academic staff and the institution.

🖨️

2. Printing difficulties

Consequence: Forms may not be printed correctly or may need to be reprinted.

Affected: Students and administrative staff.

❌

3. Incorrect information

Consequence: Wrong course codes or timetable details can cause repeated corrections.

Affected: Students, advisers and academic departments.

⏳

4. Approval delays

Consequence: Students may wait for an adviser to review and sign the physical form.

Affected: Students and academic advisers.

💾

5. Repeated data entry

Consequence: Manual entry increases workload and the possibility of data-entry errors.

Affected: Administrative officers, students and the institution.

D • STUDENT B

Precautions & Warnings

⚠ Avoid peak-time delays

Avoid waiting until the last minute to begin registration.

⚠ Do not submit unchecked information

Do not submit a form before checking course codes and timetable details.

⚠ Make sure your details are correct

Make sure personal and course information is accurate before submission.

⚠ Avoid unnecessary reprinting

Avoid printing a new form until all corrections have been confirmed.

⚠ Remember to check approval status

Remember to monitor the registration status and respond promptly to requests for correction.

F • STUDENT D

Passive Voice Process Explanation

First, the integrated registration system is accessed by the student and the required courses are selected online. Next, the course codes, timetable conflicts and course availability are checked automatically by the system. Then, any errors or conflicts are corrected online before the registration is submitted electronically. After that, a digital notification is sent to the academic adviser, and the registration is reviewed and approved online. The approved information is then updated automatically in the institutional database. Finally, an immediate confirmation is sent to the student and the registration is completed within the fifteen-minute target.

Less paper

🤖

Automatic checking

🔔

Faster approval

🔄

Less repeated work

Immediate confirmation
